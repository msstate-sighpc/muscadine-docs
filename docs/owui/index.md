# Open WebUI

Open WebUI is a tool that allows you to interact with AI models through a web interface. It's very similar to most other AI websites such as ChatGPT, however it uses our own locally hosted model on Muscadine.

## Accessing

All members with Muscadine access will also have access to Open WebUI by default. To access Open WebUI, you can go to https://muscadine-llm.hpc.msstate.edu and use the "Continue with OIDC" button to authenticate with your HPC2 account.

```{note}
To visit the Open WebUI page, you must be connected to the MSU HPC VPN. For more information, look here: {ref}`VPN setup steps <method-two-hpc-vpn>`
```

## Using

Once you have logged into Open WebUI, you will see a chat interface and a sidebar containing your previous chat history. In the top left you will have a dropdown allowing you to switch between the regular LLM and Ripple, which is the cluster AI agent powered by Hermes. Currently, our LLM model is `gpt-oss-20b`, however we are looking into other models and welcome any suggestions that you may have.
