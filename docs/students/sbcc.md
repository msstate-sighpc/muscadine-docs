# Single Board Cluster Competition

The Single Board Cluster Competition is a yearly competition hosted by the University of California San Diego during the spring focused on designing, building, configuring, and benchmarking an HPC cluster using commercially available single-board computers. The competition challenges teams to get the most efficient performance out of their clusters while staying under a $6,000 price limit and 250W power limit.

## Team History

2026 was the first year that MSU participated in SBCC. The 2026 SBCC ran from April 9th - 11th.
