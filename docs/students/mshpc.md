The Mississippi High Performance Computing (MSHPC) Conference was formed to bring together HPC professionals, researchers, and support staff of any discipline working with advanced computing. Additionally, this conference is open to students from around the state who have an interest for HPC. We here at Mississippi State University put together a group of students who have shown extreme interest in HPC and commute together to MSHPC Conference to represent our organization and share what we are doing as students.

The MSHPC Conference is held annually at Jackson State University's College of Science, Engineering and Technology.
